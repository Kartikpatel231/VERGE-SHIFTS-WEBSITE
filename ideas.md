# Verge Shifts — Design Brief

## Ground-truth reference
The supplied screenshot is the ground-truth spec. Match its composition, content hierarchy, premium editorial tone, and calm transformation-consulting atmosphere.

## Design movement
Editorial strategy studio: quiet confidence, generous whitespace, structured grids, and cinematic landscape photography.

## Core principles
- **Navigate complexity**: every section should feel like a clear next step.
- **Premium restraint**: minimal ornament, precise typography, thin rules, and considered spacing.
- **Human + systems**: pair strategic language with a warm founder story.
- **From boardroom to digital**: combine executive credibility with approachable clarity.

## Color philosophy
- Deep ink teal: `#0e3941`
- Soft seafoam: `#e8f0ed`
- Warm white: `#f8f8f5`
- Forest accent: `#1d6e6b`
- Muted slate: `#617378`
- Fine line: `#d6e1df`

## Layout paradigm
Full-width sections with an 82% max-width inner frame. Desktop uses an asymmetrical editorial grid; mobile collapses to a single column while preserving hierarchy. Hero is a full-bleed panoramic image with left-aligned copy.

## Signature elements
- Split geometric V mark for the brand
- Thin uppercase eyebrow labels with generous letter spacing
- Small arrow glyphs on cards and buttons
- Circular numbered approach steps
- Image crops with teal overlays and subtle texture

## Interaction philosophy
Quiet, purposeful micro-interactions: underlines on navigation, lift-on-hover cards, image zoom on journeys, and a mobile navigation drawer.

## Animation
Use only short fade/translate entrances and hover transitions; no distracting loops.

## Typography system
Use `Manrope` for display/body, with uppercase tracking for labels. Headlines are large, light-to-medium weight, and tightly set. Body copy is muted slate with comfortable line-height.

## Brand voice
Clear, capable, human, and forward-looking. Never overstate. Prefer verbs such as navigate, align, design, enable, and shape.

## Wordmark/logo
A custom geometric V mark in deep teal paired with a two-line Verge Shifts wordmark. The footer repeats the identity.

## Signature brand color
Deep ink teal `#0e3941`, balanced by seafoam panels and warm-white page surfaces.

## Assets
The page uses replaceable remote image URLs for a mountain hero, four journey cards, a founder portrait, and the closing CTA image. Swap these URLs for uploaded assets later without changing layout code.
